#### **🎨 About the Archive**
Operating as an independent boutique press and creative studio, the archive bridges contemporary art, literature, and open science. This repository serves as an approachable launchpad, translating complex cosmic concepts into low-barrier physics tutorials and hands-on stellar experiments.

Our broader publishing scope and interdisciplinary works debuted publicly during **[CT Tech Week](https://cttechweek.com/)** at the Ely Center for Contemporary Art. To honor the spirit of shared knowledge, these digital blueprints and layout guides are completely open under our **[CC BY 4.0 Legal License](https://creativecommons.org)**.

### 🚀 Open-Source Printables & Creative STEM Crafts
These quick, beginner-friendly tutorial blueprints demonstrate how real space concepts work using light, imagination, and simple crafting supplies.

| Released Edition | The Creative Concept (How it Works!) | Try the Experience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ALPHA** <br> *1-Min Lightsail* | **Sailing on Light:** Craft an index card solar sail powered by a standard flashlight. Discover how future spacecraft glide across the cosmos using the continuous momentum of bouncing light particles. | 🌌 [Launch Project](https://cyber-chic.github.io/cosmos/spacecraft) |
| **BETA** <br> *10-Sec Photon Rocket* | **Action & Reaction:** Assemble a solid-state LED battery throwie to simulate deep-space light engines. Demonstrates foundational propulsion laws using pure light instead of heavy chemical fuel. | 🌌 [Launch Project](https://cyber-chic.github.io/cosmos/rockets) <br><br> ⚠️ **Safety:** *Lithium core hazard. Supervise strictly; keep away from small pets/children.* |
| **GAMMA** <br> *Invisible Ink Capsule* | **Secret Data Profiles:** Write hidden messages or poetry using an invisible ink pen. Project a mini ultraviolet light beam over the canvas to watch glowing compounds instantly flash into view. | 🌌 [Launch Project](https://cyber-chic.github.io/cosmos/cosmic-capsule) |
