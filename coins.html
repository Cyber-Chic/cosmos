How the Coin Hunt & Honor System Work
Each coin is a hand-inked physical micro-edition released into independent art spaces worldwide. While its front face is photographed and sent to space as an archival print, the reverse poetry fragment stays earthbound for in-person finders. In return for keeping your coin, we simply ask that you pass along one quiet act of kindness.
